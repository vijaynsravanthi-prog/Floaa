# FLOAA Engineering Principles

## Core Principles

1. Google Sheets is the single source of truth.
2. Prefer simple solutions over scalable ones until required.
3. Manual operations are acceptable for low-frequency business processes.
4. Reuse existing code before creating new abstractions.
5. Avoid introducing new infrastructure without measurable business value.
6. Document technical debt instead of implementing speculative solutions.
7. Optimize for maintainability and business velocity.
